## How to Use This Sheet

Fill in one row per day. Keep it simple — a few numbers and one note. The point is to see trends over time, not to record everything.

**Column guide:**
- **Date** — day you are recording
- **Visitors** — total visitors that day (from Google Analytics, or estimate if not set up yet)
- **Top article** — which article got the most visits
- **Affiliate clicks** — how many people clicked a deal link
- **Conversions** — how many people actually bought/booked something
- **Earnings (USD)** — money earned that day
- **Notes** — one line about what changed or what you noticed
